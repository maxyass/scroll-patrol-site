Move first. Scroll second.

Scroll Patrol puts the apps you lose hours to behind a workout. They stay locked until you have minutes to spend on them — and the only way to get minutes is to move.

Download on the App Store Support & Privacy

Requires iOS 17.6 or later. No account, no subscription.

How it works

Three steps, and then it runs itself.

  1. Put the right apps on patrol

    Pick any apps from your library. They lock straight away, and stay locked until there are minutes on them.

  2. Earn minutes by moving

    Prop your phone up and do a set — push-ups, squats, jumping jacks or a plank, counted by the camera as you go. Or walk, and let your steps turn into minutes in the background.

  3. Spend them where they matter

    Everything you earn lands in one balance. Give an app fifteen minutes, keep the rest for later, or spend the lot in one go.

What you get

Built around one idea: the number of minutes you have is the number that matters.

Reps counted on camera

Push-ups, squats, jumping jacks and planks are counted automatically while you move. No wearable, no manual logging, no honour system.

Walking counts too

Steps and distance come straight from Apple Health and become minutes in the background. Nothing to start, nothing to remember.

See the time running down

Every patrolled app shows how many minutes it has left, and warns you a minute before it locks. Adding time tops up what is already there.

Nothing leaves your phone

No account, no sign-up, no server. Camera frames are read on device and discarded. There is no analytics code and no third-party software in the app.

Honest every morning

Balances reset at midnight, so a day you earned nothing starts at nothing. Streaks only continue if you actually showed up yesterday.

Readable at any size

Every screen supports Dynamic Type, so the app respects the text size you already chose in iOS rather than overriding it.

We collect nothing.

Scroll Patrol has no backend, no account system and no analytics. Your workout history, your Health data and your choice of apps stay on your device, and deleting the app removes all of it. We have nothing to sell because we never receive anything.
