Getting Scroll Patrol working

How does the app actually work?

You choose which apps go on patrol. Those apps stay locked until you give them minutes, and the only way to get minutes is to move — push-ups, squats, jumping jacks and planks counted by the camera, or steps and walking distance read from Apple Health.

Earned minutes land in a single balance. You decide which app gets them and how many.

The camera isn't counting my reps

Nearly always one of three things:

  • Framing. Stand your phone upright, leaning against something, with your whole body in the shot. The app shows a framing check before a set starts — wait for it to go green.
  • Direction. Push-ups and planks are counted with your body pointed towards the camera, not side-on. Squats and jumping jacks work facing the camera.
  • Light. Counting is done by reading your body's position, so a dim room or a bright window directly behind you will cost you reps.

Reps are also counted deliberately generously, so a rep that doesn't quite reach full depth should still register.

My steps aren't turning into minutes

Open the Health app, go to your profile, then Apps & Services → Scroll Patrol, and make sure Steps and Walking + Running Distance are both switched on.

Steps are counted from the point you last claimed, not from midnight, so the number on the Cardio tab is what's waiting to be claimed rather than your whole day's total.

Apps aren't locking

Locking is built on Apple's Screen Time system, so Scroll Patrol needs that permission granted. Check Settings → Screen Time on your iPhone, and confirm the app still appears there.

If you denied the permission at first launch, iOS will not ask again — you will need to delete and reinstall the app to be asked a second time.

What happens to minutes I've already given an app?

Adding more time to an app adds to whatever is left on it, rather than replacing it. The allocation screen shows the running total before you confirm.

Locking an app early burns any minutes still sitting on it — they do not come back to your balance. The app asks you to confirm before it does that.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Everything Scroll Patrol stores lives on your device, inside the app itself, so removing it removes the lot — balance, history, streak and your patrolled-app list. There is no server copy to request, because there is no server.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone or iPad running iOS 17.6 or later. Camera-counted exercises need a working front or rear camera; walking needs Apple Health. No wearable, no account, no subscription.

Legal

Privacy Policy

Scroll Patrol collects nothing.

No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party code of any kind. The app has no networking in it — there is nowhere for your information to go.

Information we collect

None. Scroll Patrol does not ask you to create an account or provide a name, email address or phone number. It does not collect usage statistics, crash analytics, advertising identifiers or device identifiers, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Information the app uses on your device

To do its job, the app reads a few things on your iPhone. All of it stays there.

WhatWhy, and what happens to it
Camera Used only while a workout is running, to count reps and time holds. Frames are analysed in memory on your device using Apple's on-device Vision framework and are discarded immediately. No video or image is ever recorded, saved to your photo library, or transmitted.
Apple Health Step count and walking + running distance are read, with your permission, to convert walking into minutes. This is read-only — the app never writes anything to Health — and the figures stay on your device.
Screen Time Your choice of apps to patrol is handled by Apple's Family Controls system. iOS hands the app opaque tokens rather than a readable list, and your selection, along with the app names shown on lock screens and reminders, is stored only on your device.
App data Your balance, workout history, streak, reward rates and settings are saved in the app's own private storage on your device.
Notifications If you allow them, the app posts a local reminder before an app's time runs out. These are generated on your device and are not sent through any server.

Information we share

None, with anyone, for any purpose. We do not sell, rent or disclose personal information, because we never receive any. There are no advertising networks, analytics providers, or third-party software development kits embedded in the app.

Data retention and deletion

Because everything is stored locally, you are in full control of it. Deleting Scroll Patrol from your device permanently removes all of its data. We hold no backup and no copy, so there is nothing for you to request from us and nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Children

Scroll Patrol is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Privacy laws including the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export and delete personal information held about you. We hold none, so these rights are satisfied on your device: the data is already in your possession, and deleting the app erases it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on the App Store.
